I was born on the 18th of July, 2***, into the family of Pastor and Pastor (Mrs.) M. Adedayo Bayode in Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria.

My educational journey began at Cedak Nursery and Primary School. I later attended Alakunre Comprehensive High School. During my secondary-school journey, I left Alakunre at SS2 and continued my education at Unique Heritage College, starting from SS1. I did not repeat a class; rather, I continued my secondary education through a change of school. I graduated from secondary school in July 2026, and I am now building my education toward the next level.

My faith is an important part of who I am. I worship at Jesus Family Church, where my father is the founder. I am also one of the church workers and serve in different areas of the church. My Christian faith, service and desire to grow spiritually continue to shape my life.

I am a Gospel Artist and the founder and coordinator of the Rejoice Gospel Band. Music is one of the ways I express myself and serve God. What started as an idea has grown into a gospel music band that is continuing to move forward, with the vision of ministering through music with excellence and passion.

Beyond music and ministry, I have a strong interest in technology and digital creativity. I am developing my skills in graphic design, website development, app development and other areas of graphics and digital technology. I enjoy exploring new tools, building projects and learning by doing.

I would describe myself as a tech bro, creative and learner — someone who enjoys discovering how things work and turning ideas into something real.

I do not consider my journey complete. I am still learning, growing and discovering the different ways I can use my gifts, education, creativity, technology, music and faith to make an impact.

“This is only the beginning of my story.”
